### Papers Supporting Thesis Novelty

| Citation | Venue | What It Establishes | Relevance to Our Work |
|----------|-------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| Haque et al., "FedStrategist: A Meta-Learning Framework for Adaptive and Robust Aggregation in FL" | arXiv 2507.14322, Jul 2025 | Uses contextual bandit to dynamically select the optimal aggregation rule from multiple defenses based on real-time diagnostics. Identifies the "Aggregation Trilemma" (performance vs robustness vs heterogeneity). | **Complementary approach**: FedStrategist uses MAB on the **defense side** to select aggregation rules. Our work uses MAB on the **attack side** to discover defense vulnerabilities. Together they suggest MAB-based adaptive selection is effective on both sides of the adversarial dynamic. |
| Yang et al., "Enhanced Model Poisoning Attack and Multi-Strategy Defense in FL" | IEEE TIFS, vol. 20, pp. 3877-3892, 2025 | Proposes ScaleSign attack that defeats sign-statistics-based defenses (SignGuard) and cosine/distance-based aggregation. Multi-strategy defense framework. | **Validates our multi-axis approach**: Yang et al. show that single-axis attacks can be countered by combining detection signals. Our framework tests whether combining multiple attack axes (type × scheduling × layering) can overcome individual defenses. |
| Liu et al., "BadSampler: Harnessing Catastrophic Forgetting to Poison Byzantine-robust FL" | KDD 2024 | Clean-label poisoning via selective data sampling exploiting catastrophic forgetting. Bypasses FLTrust and other Byzantine-robust aggregation rules without modifying updates. | **FLTrust stress test**: BadSampler demonstrates that even FLTrust (which our experiments confirm is the most resilient defense) can be defeated by clean-label attacks. This motivates our framework's broader attack repertoire — if FLTrust's resilience is conditional on attack type, multi-axis probing is essential. |
| Molina-Coronado, "Celtibero: Robust Layered Aggregation for FL" | arXiv 2408.14240, Aug 2024 | Introduces layered aggregation combining multiple defense signals. Outperforms FL-Defender, LFighter, FLAME. Effective under non-IID. | **Layered defense vs layered attack**: Celtibero proposes layering defense mechanisms. Our framework tests whether layered/composite attacks can defeat individual defenses — a natural adversarial complement to layered defenses. |
